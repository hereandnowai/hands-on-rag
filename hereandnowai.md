# HERE AND NOW AI

## Overview

HERE AND NOW AI is an Artificial Intelligence Research Institute based in Chennai, India. It focuses on AI education, applied research, AI agents, automation systems, and AI-first software development.

Founded by Ruthran Raghavan as an evolution of his long-standing educational initiatives that began in 2011, HERE AND NOW AI is committed to democratizing Artificial Intelligence for students, professionals, institutions, and enterprises.

The institute operates at the intersection of research, training, and real-world AI deployment — ensuring measurable, practical AI transformation rather than theoretical adoption.

---

## Location

HERE AND NOW AI is headquartered in **Chennai, India**.

It collaborates with academic institutions, corporates, and organizations across regions through training programs, research partnerships, and AI solution deployments.

There are no official headquarters in Austin, Texas or satellite offices in London.

---

## Products and Services

1. **AI Education & Training Programs**
   Structured AI programs for colleges, universities, corporates, and professionals — covering Generative AI, AI agents, automation, and AI-first software development.

2. **AI Agents & Automation Systems**
   Custom AI agents, MCP servers and clients, and intelligent automation systems designed to streamline workflows and reduce manual intervention.

3. **AI-First Software Development**
   Building scalable AI-powered applications including AI-powered CRM systems, AI platforms, and domain-specific intelligent tools.

4. **Research & Applied AI Innovation**
   Development of advanced AI systems, including the long-term vision of building a Fully Autonomous AI Professor and AI-driven educational ecosystems.
